TNPSC exam preparation in Tamil

GoKalvi TNPSC helps you prepare for the TNPSC Group 4, Group 2/2A and VAO exams in Tamil and English. Practise previous years’ Group 4 questions with answers checked against TNPSC’s published answer keys, study General Tamil grammar and literature lessons, read the Indian Constitution article by article, and revise current affairs every month. Lessons follow the Samacheer Kalvi textbooks for Classes 6 to 12. Free for everyone until the Group 4 exam on 10 January 2027.
