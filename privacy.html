1. Introduction

Gokalvi is a fully offline AI tutor for TNPSC (Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission) aspirants. This Privacy Policy explains what data Gokalvi collects, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Short version: Gokalvi is designed to work entirely offline. We do not collect or share personal information. The AI on your device stays on your device.

2. What the app does NOT do

  • Does not collect your name, phone number, email address, or location
  • Does not require account registration or sign-in
  • Does not show advertisements
  • Does not track your behaviour for analytics or profiling
  • Does not sell or share data with third parties
  • Does not upload your notes, study history, or quiz results to any server
  • Does not use cookies

3. Data stored on your device

Gokalvi stores the following data locally on your device only. No part of this data leaves your device.

All data is stored in the app's private storage. It is not accessible to other apps on your device. You can export it at any time using the Backup & Restore feature, or delete it by clearing app data.

4. Camera and Microphone

Gokalvi requests optional permission to use your Camera and Microphone for the Second Brain note-taking feature:

  • Camera: Used to capture images and attach them to your study notes. Photos are stored locally in the app's private storage. They are never uploaded.
  • Microphone: Used to record voice notes. Recordings are processed entirely on-device using the local AI model for transcription. Audio is stored locally. It is never uploaded.

Both permissions are off by default. The app will ask for your permission the first time you use these features. You can revoke permission at any time via Android Settings → Apps → Gokalvi → Permissions.

5. Internet access

Gokalvi's core features — AI tutoring, PYQ search, aptitude drills, Treasure knowledge packs, Study Planner — work without internet access.

Gokalvi requests internet access for one optional feature only:

Current Affairs updates: Gokalvi can download new Current Affairs packs to keep your preparation current. This is a user-initiated action; the app does not fetch data in the background automatically. When you trigger an update, the app connects to Gokalvi's secure servers to download a content pack. The only information sent is the version number of your current pack so the server knows what to send back. No personal identifiers are transmitted.

6. Purchases (In-App)

Gokalvi offers optional paid features via Google Play's billing system. All payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play. Gokalvi does not collect, see, or store your payment card details. For questions about billing, refer to Google Play's Privacy Policy.

7. Children's Privacy

Gokalvi is intended for adults preparing for TNPSC examinations (minimum eligibility age is 18 years). It is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, please contact us at admin@gokalvi.com.

8. Data Security

All data stored on your device is held in the app's private storage directory, which is inaccessible to other apps without root access. You may optionally enable database encryption via Settings → Data & Security. Backups you create are stored in a location you choose on your device or external storage.

9. Your Choices and Rights

  • Delete all app data: Android Settings → Apps → Gokalvi → Clear Data
  • Delete specific notes: Second Brain → Notes Manager → select items → delete
  • Export your data: Settings → Backup & Restore → Export
  • Manage corpus data: Settings → Data & Storage → Storage Budget
  • Revoke Camera permission: Android Settings → Apps → Gokalvi → Permissions → Camera
  • Revoke Microphone permission: Android Settings → Apps → Gokalvi → Permissions → Microphone

10. Changes to This Policy
